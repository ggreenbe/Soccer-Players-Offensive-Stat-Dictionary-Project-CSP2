# Soccer Players Dictionary

### One-Sentence Description:
A dictionary including legends and rising stars in the soccer world with a menu to view the whole list, calculate stat ratings, find averages across positions, find the most outstanding players in a category, and more!

### Calculations:
The calculations in this app include:
- Career stat ratings
- Average stats in a category (ie, average goals in legend forwards or average trophies in star midfielders)
- The player with the most of a certain stat in a category (goals, assists, trophies, clubs)

### Challenges:
The biggest challenge I ran into was finding a way to accurately portray a player's career rating based on solely goals, assists, and trophies. At first, I tried giving each player an equal calculation, but that didn't work, as forwards were much more stat-heavy than many midfielders. This resulted in a very high rating for the forwards with a very low rating for the midfielders. Additionally, many current players were at a disadvantage compared to the retired legends, as the legends had many more stats because of their completed careers. This gave me an idea: to weigh the stats of different categories differently. I decided to give current midfielders much more inflation, as they are the least stat-heavy group (I only included forward and midfielder stats in this project). I then gave the lowest inflation to the retired forwards, who are the most stat-heavy. This helped me get much closer to a better rating system, but it is still far from perfect.

### What I Would Improve With More Time:
If I had more time, I would aim to improve the rating system. The best way to do this would be to add more stats to each player and focus on their season averages rather than their totals. My biggest issue is that players like midfielders who control games rather than rack up their goals and assists don't receive the credit they deserve in this system, nor do younger players.
